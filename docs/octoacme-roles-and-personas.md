# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

## Additional Cross-Functional Roles

These roles provide focused expertise across discovery, delivery, and release. A project may assign them to dedicated people or combine them with existing roles based on its size and needs. The Project Manager coordinates their delivery dependencies; the Product Manager remains accountable for product priorities and outcomes.

### UX / Product Designers

#### Role Summary
UX / Product Designers research user needs and shape accessible, usable product experiences.

#### Responsibilities
- Plan and synthesize user research with the Product Manager
- Create and validate user flows, interaction designs, and prototypes
- Define usability and accessibility considerations for acceptance criteria
- Share design decisions and open questions with the delivery team

#### Collaboration
- Partner with the Product Manager to connect user needs to product goals and priorities
- Work with Developers to assess feasibility and resolve design questions during implementation
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on usability and accessibility checks
- Share research findings and design decisions with the Project Manager for planning and stakeholder updates

### Data / Analytics Leads

#### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads define how project outcomes will be measured and ensure the data used for decisions is trustworthy.

#### Responsibilities
- Translate success metrics into instrumentation and reporting requirements
- Define event, data-quality, and measurement plans
- Validate that reported results support product and delivery decisions
- Communicate measurement limitations and findings

#### Collaboration
- Work with the Product Manager to align measures with intended customer and business outcomes
- Coordinate with Developers to implement instrumentation and with QA/Testing to verify it
- Provide the Project Manager with progress and measurement risks that affect milestones or reporting
- Share findings with Stakeholders to inform decisions and follow-up work

### Security / Privacy Leads

#### Role Summary
Security / Privacy Leads advise teams on security, privacy, and applicable compliance requirements throughout delivery.

#### Responsibilities
- Identify security and privacy risks and recommend mitigations
- Advise on data handling, access, and retention requirements
- Review relevant designs and requirements early enough to inform implementation
- Define or coordinate security and privacy validation needs, and escalate material concerns

#### Collaboration
- Partner with Developers and QA/Testing on secure design, implementation, and validation
- Advise the Product Manager on risk and requirements affecting scope or prioritization
- Work with the Project Manager to track mitigations, dependencies, and escalations
- Inform Stakeholders of material risks through the agreed escalation path

### Operations / Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) Leads

#### Role Summary
Operations / SRE Leads help ensure services can be deployed, observed, supported, and operated reliably.

#### Responsibilities
- Define relevant reliability, availability, monitoring, and alerting expectations
- Identify operational dependencies and release-readiness requirements
- Coordinate runbooks, support handoffs, and incident-response readiness
- Review operational signals after release and surface reliability risks

#### Collaboration
- Work with Developers on service design, observability, and operational readiness
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on release and smoke-test expectations
- Partner with the Project Manager to plan operational dependencies and communicate readiness or blockers
- Advise the Product Manager and Stakeholders on reliability trade-offs and post-release impact

### Change Management / Communications Leads

#### Role Summary
Change Management / Communications Leads prepare affected audiences to understand, adopt, and support project changes.

#### Responsibilities
- Identify audiences affected by a change and their information or training needs
- Plan and coordinate launch communications, training, and adoption support
- Gather feedback and adoption signals after rollout
- Keep messaging aligned with the release scope and known limitations

#### Collaboration
- Coordinate timing, audiences, and status messages with the Project Manager
- Work with the Product Manager to communicate customer value, intended outcomes, and product changes
- Partner with Stakeholders and support teams on readiness, training, and feedback collection
- Confirm with Developers and QA/Testing that release details and known issues are represented accurately
